About

A telecommunications researcher working across network systems and data science.

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Strasbourg, conducting research at the ICube Laboratory on Digital Twins for efficient wireless networks.

My path through Syria, Italy, and France has shaped a multidisciplinary approach spanning embedded systems, network intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, and cloud/edge computing.

My goal is to turn network data into digital replicas that can explain, predict, and improve the systems they represent.
